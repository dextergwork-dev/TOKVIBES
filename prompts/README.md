| File | Used by |
|---|---|
| `client-briefing-master.md` | The 8 daily client briefing routines (shared instructions) |
| `client-configs.md` | Per-client config blocks, appended to the master for each routine |
| `ops-update-for-oli.md` | Weekday 10:00 AM ET Ops Update draft for Oli in #ops-team |

## Schedule
- Client briefings: daily, ~6:30-7:05 AM ET (UTC 10:30-11:05; shifts an hour earlier in Eastern after the Nov 1 daylight saving change). Delivered as a Slack DM to Dexter.
- Ops Update: weekdays 10:00 AM ET (UTC 14:00 now, 15:00 after Nov 1). Saved as a Slack draft in #ops-team, never posted.

## Behavior
- Client channel updates: Mon/Wed/Fri only. Strategist and editor updates: every day.
- Sources: Slack, ClickUp, Notion, Gmail, Fireflies meetings (all read-only).
- Routines never post to client/strategy/editing channels and never change ClickUp or Notion.
