# TokVibes routine prompts (backup)

Backup copies of the prompts used by Dexter's cloud routines (managed at https://claude.ai/code/routines). The routines themselves are NOT stored in this repo; edit them in Claude, then update these files.

| File | Used by |
|---|---|
| `client-briefing-master.md` | The 8 daily client briefing routines (shared instructions) |
| `client-configs.md` | Per-client config blocks, appended to the master for each routine |
| `ops-update-for-oli.md` | Weekday 10:00 AM ET Ops Update draft for Oli in #ops-team |
| `strategist-wednesday-update.md` | Wednesday 11:00 AM ET message to strategists in #tokvibes-a-team |

## Schedule (weekdays only for briefings)
- Client briefings: Mon-Fri, ~6:30-7:05 AM ET (UTC 10:30-11:05; shifts an hour earlier in Eastern after the Nov 1 daylight saving change). Delivered as a Slack DM to Dexter.
- Ops Update: Mon-Fri 10:00 AM ET (UTC 14:00 now, 15:00 after Nov 1). Saved as a Slack draft in #ops-team, never posted.
- Strategist Update: Wednesdays 11:00 AM ET (UTC 15:00 now). Posts automatically to #tokvibes-a-team (authorized by Dexter) and DMs Dexter a copy.

## Behavior
- Client channel updates: Mon/Wed/Fri only. Strategist and editor updates: every day.
- Sources: Slack, ClickUp (incl. Billing Queue, Bills to Pay, Ops Tasks, Strategist Delivery Tracker), Notion, Gmail, Fireflies meetings (all read-only).
- Briefings and the Ops Update never post to client/strategy/editing channels and never change ClickUp or Notion.
